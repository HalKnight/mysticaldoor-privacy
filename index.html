Inside every book

  • Tactical combat

    Turn-based battles on an isometric grid, with terrain, cooldowns, critical hits, knockbacks and status effects to play around.

  • Mysteries you can solve

    The evidence is placed in the world, not handed to you, and deduction boards ask you to make sense of what you have found.

  • Fully voiced

    Every chapter is narrated, and every character speaks their lines in conversation.

  • Worlds to explore

    More than a hundred hand-built places, with rain, snow, fog, embers and fireflies, and a map that fills in as you go.

  • Gear and growth

    Six tiers of weapons, armour and trinkets, levels that make you stronger, and abilities to rank up.

  • Your pace

    Three difficulty levels change how hard foes hit, never what you earn. Switch any time.
